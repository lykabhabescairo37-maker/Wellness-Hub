# Wellness Hub

An all-in-one healthy living website. Log your food, follow indoor workouts with a built-in timer, track daily habits, plan weekly goals, and write a short mood journal.

## Features
- Home dashboard with today's stats and weekly charts
- Food log with a daily calorie total (you enter your own numbers)
- 5 indoor workout routines with a step-by-step timer
- Water, sleep, and movement habit tracking
- Weekly goals checklist
- Mood journal
- Everything is saved in your browser, no account needed
- Works on mobile

## Usage
Open `index.html` in your browser.

*A general wellness tool, not medical advice. Stop any exercise if something hurts.*
